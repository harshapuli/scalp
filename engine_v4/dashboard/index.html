📖 What do the 6 signals mean? Click to expand
1. Compression 0–20 pts
Price tightening over 10 days — a coiled spring before expansion. Lit when daily range < 3-7% of price. Mega-caps in trend rarely show this; mostly fires on consolidating stocks.
2. Persistent Flow 0–25 pts
Net call (or put) ASK-side premium over 5 consecutive days. Large, repeated institutional bets — not a single sweep. Strongest single signal when lit. Direction determined here.
3. Dark Pool 0–15 pts
Multi-day institutional block prints at a cluster price, validated 0–4/4: (≥3 days of prints) + (size ≥ 5% ADV) + (spot holds above the cluster) + (support tested). Quiet accumulation footprint.
4. Sector RS 0–15 pts
Ticker's 5-day return vs SPY. Lit when outperforming by 0.5%+; full points at >3%. Money rotating IN means this name is leading, not lagging.
5. GEX Regime 0–15 pts
Where today's aggregate dealer gamma exposure sits in its 1-year distribution. Short gamma (negative or bottom 20%) = amplification regime = breakout-friendly (+15). Top 70%+ = HEAVY_LONG = dealers suppress moves (0 pts). Today most mega-caps are 95-99th percentile long — retail FOMO is fighting dealer walls.
6. Skew Flat 0–10 pts
Difference between 25-delta put IV and 25-delta call IV, normalized by ATM IV. Flat skew (<3%) = market not paying for downside protection = risk-on confirmation (+10). Steep skew (>15%) = fear premium in puts, bullish fighting headwind (0 pts).
Score threshold: 40 out of 100. A candidate at 40 has ~2-3 lit signals — enough for a watch, not enough for conviction. Most actionable setups land at 50+. The killer combo: Persistent Flow + multi-day Dark Pool + short-gamma regime + flat skew — all four confirming = real institutional positioning.
